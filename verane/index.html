The atelier

See the watches

Two hundred and twelve parts, one steady beat.

Each calibre is built from brass, steel and synthetic ruby, then regulated on the bench until it keeps time within a few seconds a day. Nothing is hidden behind the dial that we would not show you.

Parts
212 per movement
Jewels
31 synthetic rubies
Power reserve
72 hours

Every edge is touched by a person.

Cases are brushed and polished on the wheel, hands are set under a loupe, and straps are cut, stitched and fitted by the same pair of hands that will check the watch before it ships.

Finishing
Brushed and polished by hand
Per watch
One watchmaker, start to finish
Final check
Ten days of rate testing
